5. Features / Highlights 
The best dashboard explanation format.1. Business Problem: Difficulty in tracking and analyzing intern information efficiently.2. Goal: Create a simple dashboard for quick monitoring and analysis.3. Key Visuals: KPI cards, charts, tables, and interactive filters.4. Business Impact: Helps management understand intern distribution, track internship activities, and make data-driven decisions.

Example :1. Business Problem Managing intern records manually makes it difficult to track intern details, attendance, departments, and internship progress efficiently.

Key Visuals
Total Interns – Displays the overall number of interns.
Interns by Department – Shows department-wise intern distribution.
Attendance Analysis – Tracks intern attendance and working days.
Internship Status – Shows active/completed internship status.
Intern Details Table – Provides important information about each intern.

1. Business Impact & Insights
Helps management track interns efficiently.
Provides quick insights into attendance and department distribution.
Reduces manual work and improves data-based decision making.
Makes it easier to monitor internship progress and status.
